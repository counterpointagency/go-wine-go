Why direct

The shortest line between a vineyard and your table

Most Australian wine reaches you through three or four sets of hands, and each one takes a margin. Buying direct removes them. The winery sets a price, you make an offer, and the difference stays with the two of you.

Growers keep more of what their fruit is worth. You pay closer to what the wine actually costs to make. Nothing is bought on your behalf, held in a warehouse, or marked up before you see it.

How it works

Three steps, and your money never moves early

  1. 01

    Browse wines

    Listings come straight from the winery. Filter by subregion, variety or price. No resellers, no markups.

  2. 02

    Make your offer

    Name your price per case. The winery can accept, counter or decline. Nothing is charged while you negotiate.

  3. 03

    Wine delivered

    Your payment sits in escrow while the winery ships direct. Funds release only once delivery is confirmed.
